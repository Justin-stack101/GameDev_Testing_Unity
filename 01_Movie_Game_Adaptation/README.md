# Movie / Film to Video Game Adaptation

- **Selected Film**: *Mad Max: Fury Road* (2015)
- **Adapted Game Title**: *Fury Road: Citadel Convoy*
- **Grading Breakdown**: 2 Items $\times$ 25 Points = **50 Points**

---

## 1. Story Elements (25 Points)

| Element | Adaptation Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Premise** | In a sun-scorched post-apocalyptic wasteland where water and gasoline ("Guzzoline") are monopolized by a tyrannical warlord, a haunted drifter and a rebel War Rig commander form an uneasy alliance to smuggle five captive women across a ruthless desert highway—and ultimately fight their way back to liberate the Citadel itself. |
| **Backstory** | Following ecological and nuclear collapse, civilization fell into resource wars. Max Rockatansky, a former Main Force Patrol officer, lost his family to marauding gangs and wanders the wasteland haunted by survivors' guilt. Meanwhile, Immortan Joe seized the Citadel's aquifer, ruling the impoverished "Wretched" via a water monopoly and a fanatical army of "War Boys" promised rebirth in Valhalla. Imperator Furiosa, abducted as a child from the "Green Place of Many Mothers," rose through Joe's ranks while secretly plotting an escape. |
| **Synopsis** | Captured by War Boys as a universal blood donor ("Blood Bag") for Nux, Max is strapped to a pursuit car when Furiosa betrays Immortan Joe by diverting her armored War Rig off-route with Joe's five prized "Wives." After surviving a toxic sandstorm, Max frees himself and reluctantly teams up with Furiosa and the Wives to outrun three combined war parties (the Citadel, Gas Town, and the Bullet Farm). Discovering the Green Place has decayed into a toxic swamp, Max convinces Furiosa and the surviving Vuvalini warriors to turn the War Rig around and race backward through Joe's armada to capture the undefended Citadel. |
| **Theme** | Redemption over mere survival, bodily autonomy (*"We are not things!"*), breaking cycles of fanaticism, and reclaiming hope by confronting tyranny head-on rather than fleeing into an idealized past. |
| **Setting** | Mid-21st-century post-collapse Australian wasteland featuring five playable zones: **The Citadel** (vertical rock mesas & hydro-lifts), **Fury Road & Canyon of the Rock Riders**, **The Toxic Storm Zone**, **The Dead Marsh** (nocturnal stilt wetlands), and **The Salt Flats Edge**. |
| **Plot** | **Act I (Escape & Storm)**: Furiosa diverts the War Rig; Joe mobilizes his armada; Max escapes during a sandstorm and forms a truce with Furiosa.<br>**Act II (The Gauntlet & Disillusionment)**: The crew bargains with the Canyon Rock Riders, repels Joe's pursuit fleet, loses Angharad, and reaches the Vuvalini—only to learn the Green Place is dead.<br>**Act III (The U-Turn & Liberation)**: Max proposes retaking the Citadel. Joined by the reformed War Boy Nux and the Vuvalini bikers, they fight back through the canyon pass, defeat Immortan Joe, and open the Citadel's water valves to the people. |
| **Balancing Conflict** | Balances three interlocking layers: **(1) External Vehicular Combat** (convoy ambushes, pole-vaulting boarders, harpoons), **(2) Environmental Hazards** (sandstorms, overheating engines, deep mud traps, rockslides), and **(3) Internal Trust & Resource Tension** (scarce ammo, water/coolant reserves, and War Rig repairs while protecting the Wives). |
| **Shifting Focus** | Playable perspective dynamically shifts between **Max** (close-quarters brawling, interceptor combat, point-defense sniping), **Furiosa** (heavy War Rig piloting, precision long-range marksmanship, convoy tactics), and **Nux** (high-risk mechanical repairs on moving vehicles and crowd control). |
| **Suspension of Disbelief** | Players embrace operatic wasteland spectacles—such as the flame-throwing guitarist on the Doof Wagon, pole-vaulting boarders swinging at 60 mph, and twin-V8 hot-rods surviving canyon leaps—because the game's audiovisual presentation and cult lore ground these machines as sacred war relics. |
| **Realism** | Anchored by realistic physical systems: vehicle weight, torque, sand vs. mud tire traction, fuel consumption, and engine temperature. Weapons use scarce, manually counted ammunition (e.g., a 4-shell shotgun or stabilized sniper rifle), and injuries require field bandages or transfusions rather than instant regeneration. |

## 2. Characters (25 Points)

| Archetype | Character(s) & Role in Game |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hero** | **Max Rockatansky & Imperator Furiosa** (Co-Heroes). Max is the reluctant survivor who regains his humanity through selfless action; Furiosa is the visionary liberator fighting for redemption and freedom. |
| **Shadow** | **Immortan Joe**. The dark mirror of leadership: where Furiosa shares water and fights to free life, Joe hoards resources, enslaves people, and sends brainwashed youth to die for a false paradise. |
| **Mentor** | **The Keeper of the Seeds** (Elder of the Vuvalini / Many Mothers). Preserves heirloom plant seeds in a wasteland of bullets and teaches the crew that survival means planting life where water exists. |
| **Ally** | **The Five Wives** (*Toast the Knowing, Capable, The Dag, Cheedo the Fragile, The Splendid Angharad*) & **The Vuvalini Warriors**. Toast reloads weapons and drives; Capable rehabilitates Nux; the Vuvalini provide flanking motorcycle support. |
| **Guardian** | **The Rock Rider Chief & The Organic Mechanic**. Threshold guardians who block the canyon pass and test the crew's bargaining and combat resolve. |
| **Trickster** | **The Doof Warrior & Slit**. Inject chaotic sonic energy, unpredictable battlefield hazards, and manic disruption during high-speed chases. |
| **Herald** | **Furiosa's sudden left turn off the Gas Town road / Citadel Watchtower flares**. Signals the call to adventure that shatters the status quo. |
| **Protagonist** | **Imperator Furiosa** (primary narrative driver whose moral choice initiates the plot) paired with **Max Rockatansky** (viewpoint survivor whose Act III plan resolves the climax). |
| **Antagonist — Transformational** | **Nux**. Starts as a fanatical War Boy desperate to kill Furiosa and die *"shiny and chrome,"* but after failing Joe and receiving compassion from Capable, transforms into a heroic ally who sacrifices himself to save the convoy. |
| **Antagonist — Mistaken** | **Corpus Colossus & the Young War Boy Pups**. Raised inside Joe's cult to believe Furiosa is stealing their future, until Joe's fall exposes the lie and they willingly lower the lifts and release the water. |
| **Antagonist — Exaggerated** | **Rictus Erectus, The People Eater, and The Bullet Farmer**. Towering, grotesque embodiments of brute muscular savagery, gluttonous corporate greed, and blind trigger-happy militarism. |
| **Supporting Characters** | **The Valkyrie** (Vuvalini scout), **Morsov** (kamikaze War Boy), **Miss Giddy** (Citadel historian/teacher), and **The Wretched** (Citadel citizens). |
