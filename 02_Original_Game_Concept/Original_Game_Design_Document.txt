================================================================================
ORIGINAL VIDEO GAME CONCEPT DESIGN DOCUMENT
Project Title: "RESONANCE OF VITREA" (Glasswright Acoustic-Light Expedition)
Genre: 3D Harmonic-Refraction Puzzle & Environmental Restoration Adventure
Grading Breakdown: 6 Items x 10 Points = 60 Points
================================================================================

CONCEPT OVERVIEW:
Set inside "Vitrea," a subterranean world made entirely of blown silicate glass,
quartz filaments, and dormant acoustic pendulums, the player takes the role of an
apprentice "Glasswright." Instead of conventional weapons or combat, the player uses
a "Pitch-Chisel" and molten glass filaments to strike crystal structures at specific
musical frequencies and bend light beams across chasm-sized resonators to re-ignite
the world's frozen thermal heart.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GAMEPLAY (10 Points)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Core Gameplay Loop:
1. LISTEN & SURVEY: Enter a dormant, pitch-dark glass cavern and strike a tuning pulse
   to reveal structural resonance lines through echolocation ripples.
2. SPIN & BRIDGE: Extrude temporary liquid-glass bridges and prism nodes between
   floating quartz islands before the molten glass cools and hardens.
3. TUNE & REFRACT: Rotate prism lenses and adjust the thickness of glass chimes so
   that thermal light beams and sound waves harmonize at the exact frequency needed
   to thaw petrified flora and power ancient glass automatons.
4. STABILIZE: Dampen destructive vibrations ("Shatter-Quakes") before fragile bridges
   or resonators crack under harmonic overload.

Core Mechanics:
- Thermal Glass-Blowing Tool: Lets the player melt, stretch, or anneal glass surfaces
  in real time to form walkways, concave mirrors, or acoustic bells.
- Frequency Tuning: Players slide a harmonic collar along their staff to shift pitch
  between Low Bass (moves heavy obsidian counterweights), Mid Tenor (sustains bridge
  elasticity), and High Soprano (shatters corrupted slag crusts).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. RULES (10 Points)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Rule of Structural Resonance (The Fracture Limit):
   Every glass surface in the game has a visible "Stress Gauge" (shown via color
   iridescence). Sustaining a high-frequency vibration on thin glass for more than
   4 seconds causes it to fracture and collapse.
2. Rule of Thermal Cooling:
   Freshly spun molten-glass bridges remain malleable for 8 seconds before solidifying.
   Once cooled, their shape is locked unless reheated using a thermal light beam.
3. Rule of Conservation of Light & Pitch:
   A single thermal beam can be split across at most three prisms before its intensity
   drops below activation threshold; combining two beams of matching frequency doubles
   their range.
4. Rule of Silence Zones:
   In "Fault-Line Sanctuaries," loud impacts attract "Dissonance Mites" (crystalline
   parasites that feed on vibration). Players must switch to silent light-refraction
   only while crossing these zones.
5. No-Combat Restoration Ethos:
   Corrupted crystal constructs cannot be destroyed by brute force; they must be
   brought into harmonic phase alignment to purify and calm them.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. VICTORY AND LOSS CONDITIONS (10 Points)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Victory Conditions:
- Short-Term (Chamber Victory): Align all Harmonic Prisms and Acoustic Bells in a
  cavern to ignite its "Kiln Beacon," permanently illuminating the sector and unlocking
  the glass tramway to the next depth layer.
- Mid-Term (Stratum Victory): Restore all three regional Great Kilns (The Whispering
  Spire, The Obsidian Foundry, and The Prism Conservatory) to stabilize the upper crust.
- Ultimate Victory (Game Completion): Conduct the "Grand Symphony of Vitrea" at the
  Core Furnace—simultaneously routing thermal light from all upper strata into the
  Planetary Pendulum to awaken the biosphere.

Loss Conditions:
- Structural Collapse (Local Failure): Over-vibrating the floor beneath the player or
  allowing a Shatter-Quake to reach 100% stress shatters the platform, dropping the
  player into the Silicate Abyss (respawns at the chamber's last lit Kiln Beacon).
- Thermal Extinguishment (Time/Resource Failure): If the player's portable Ember-Core
  cools to 0% while away from a light beam for over 90 seconds in deep frost zones,
  their glass suit vitrifies and resets to the nearest beacon.
- Core Cracked (Stage Failure): During boss-scale Harmonic Stabilization events, if the
  central Chamber Bell sustains three un-dampened dissonance strikes, the bell cracks
  and the sequence must be restarted.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INTERACTIVITY MODES (10 Points)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. First-Person Precision Tuning Mode:
   Used when aiming light prisms, reading stress fractures through the player's
   spectroscopic visor, and striking individual glass chimes with fine pitch control.
2. Third-Person Kinetic Traversal Mode:
   Automatically transitions during movement—skating across smooth spun-glass ribbons,
   gliding on thermal updrafts, and leaping between swinging pendulum platforms.
3. Tactile Blueprint / Acoustic Map Mode:
   A diegetic 3D glass-model projection held in the player's palm that displays real-time
   wave propagation across the entire cavern for macro-level puzzle planning.
4. Asynchronous Co-Op "Echo Chimes" Mode (Optional Multiplayer):
   Players can leave tuned "Resonance Vessels" in their world that appear as ghostly
   harmonic relays in other players' games, helping strangers extend light beams toward
   secret chambers.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CHALLENGES (10 Points)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Spatial-Optic Routing Puzzles:
   Bending light beams around curved crystal walls, through moving pendulum lenses,
   and across multi-level chambers without crossing destructive shadow-prism beams.
2. Real-Time Stress Management Under Pressure:
   Crossing self-made glass bridges while rhythmic "Tectonic Pulses" ripple through
   the cavern—requiring the player to strike counter-frequencies in rhythm to cancel
   out destructive waves.
3. Environmental Material Constraints:
   Adapting to three distinct glass types:
   - Soda-Lime Glass (easy to melt and shape, but shatters easily under weight).
   - Borosilicate Quartz (resists heat and vibration, reflects light cleanly).
   - Obsidian Basalt (absorbs light completely, requires bass-frequency sound to move).
4. "Harmonic Leviathan" Encounters:
   Massive, out-of-tune clockwork glass automatons whose erratic footsteps threaten to
   shatter the cavern floor. The player must climb their exterior scaffolding and re-tune
   their internal tuning forks while dodging shockwaves.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. STRUCTURE (10 Points)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Objectives:
  Structured into three tiers:
  (a) Primary Objective: Re-ignite the 5 Deep Strata Kilns to restore the Core Furnace.
  (b) Secondary Objectives: Thaw frozen "Memory Ampoules" (glass sculptures that replay
      acoustic holograms of the vanished civilization).
  (c) Mastery Objectives: Complete chambers using the minimum number of spun-glass
      filaments ("Pure Artisan" rating).

- Flow:
  Follows a rhythmic "Tension -> Resonance -> Catharsis" cadence:
  Each stratum begins in eerie, claustrophobic darkness and fragile silence (high
  tension), builds into complex multi-beam acoustic routing (cognitive engagement),
  and culminates in a musical crescendo where the entire cavern lights up in vibrant
  stained-glass colors and harmonic chords (cathartic reward).

- Duration:
  - Individual Puzzle Chambers: 10 to 15 minutes per chamber.
  - Full Stratum (Region): 1.5 to 2 hours (comprising 6–8 chambers + 1 Leviathan Kiln).
  - Complete Main Campaign: 8 to 10 hours (12–14 hours for 100% Memory Ampoule completion).

- Availability:
  Designed as a Single-Player PC & Console release (built in Unity 3D) with optional
  online asynchronous "Echo Chimes" sharing. Playable fully offline with accessible
  visual-waveform indicators so deaf or hard-of-hearing players can read pitches
  entirely through color and geometric wave patterns.

- Relationship:
  Every chamber within a Stratum is physically and optically interconnected. Igniting
  Chamber A sends a permanent beam of light through a ceiling aperture into the central
  Hub Atrium, visibly altering the environment and unlocking mechanical counterweights
  needed in Chambers B and C.

- Progression:
  Instead of numeric XP or stat grinding, progression is tool-and-knowledge based:
  - Stratum 1 unlocks the Pitch-Chisel (Basic Resonance & Echolocation).
  - Stratum 2 unlocks the Molten Spool (Glass-Bridge Extrusion).
  - Stratum 3 unlocks the Chromatic Splitter (Splitting White Light into 3 Thermal
    Frequencies).
  - Stratum 4 unlocks the Phase-Dampener (Freezing Vibrations in Time).
  - Stratum 5 combines all four tools for the final Core Furnace symphony.
