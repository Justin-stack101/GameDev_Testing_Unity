# Original Video Game Concept Design Document

- **Project Title**: *Resonance of Vitrea*
- **Genre**: 3D Harmonic-Refraction Puzzle & Environmental Restoration Adventure
- **Grading Breakdown**: 6 Items $\times$ 10 Points = **60 Points**

---

## 1. Gameplay (10 Points)
Set inside **Vitrea**, a subterranean world made entirely of blown silicate glass, quartz filaments, and dormant acoustic pendulums, the player acts as an apprentice **Glasswright**. Instead of weapons or combat, gameplay revolves around a 4-stage harmonic loop:
1. **Listen & Survey**: Strike a tuning pulse in pitch-dark caverns to reveal structural resonance lines via echolocation ripples.
2. **Spin & Bridge**: Extrude temporary molten-glass walkways and prism nodes between floating quartz islands before the glass cools and hardens.
3. **Tune & Refract**: Rotate prism lenses and adjust glass chimes so thermal light beams and sound waves harmonize at the exact frequency needed to thaw petrified flora and power ancient glass automatons.
4. **Stabilize**: Dampen destructive vibrations (*Shatter-Quakes*) before fragile bridges or resonators crack under harmonic overload.

---

## 2. Rules (10 Points)
1. **Rule of Structural Resonance (The Fracture Limit)**: Every glass surface displays a visible *Stress Gauge* via color iridescence. Sustaining a high-frequency vibration on thin glass for more than 4 seconds causes it to fracture and collapse.
2. **Rule of Thermal Cooling**: Freshly spun molten-glass bridges remain malleable for 8 seconds before solidifying. Once cooled, their shape is locked unless reheated by a thermal light beam.
3. **Rule of Conservation of Light & Pitch**: A single thermal beam can be split across at most three prisms before dropping below activation intensity; combining two beams of matching frequency doubles their range.
4. **Rule of Silence Zones**: In *Fault-Line Sanctuaries*, loud impacts attract *Dissonance Mites* (crystalline parasites that feed on vibration). Players must switch to silent light-refraction while crossing these zones.
5. **No-Combat Restoration Ethos**: Corrupted crystal constructs cannot be destroyed by brute force; they must be brought into harmonic phase alignment to calm and purify them.

---

## 3. Victory and Loss Conditions (10 Points)
- **Victory Conditions**:
  - **Short-Term (Chamber Victory)**: Align all Harmonic Prisms and Acoustic Bells in a cavern to ignite its *Kiln Beacon*, permanently illuminating the sector and unlocking the glass tramway.
  - **Mid-Term (Stratum Victory)**: Restore all three regional Great Kilns (*The Whispering Spire*, *The Obsidian Foundry*, and *The Prism Conservatory*).
  - **Ultimate Victory (Game Completion)**: Conduct the *Grand Symphony of Vitrea* at the Core Furnace—routing thermal light from all upper strata into the Planetary Pendulum to awaken the biosphere.
- **Loss Conditions**:
  - **Structural Collapse**: Over-vibrating the floor beneath the player or letting a *Shatter-Quake* reach 100% stress shatters the platform, dropping the player into the Silicate Abyss (respawns at the last lit Kiln Beacon).
  - **Thermal Extinguishment**: Allowing the player's portable *Ember-Core* to cool to 0% away from light beams for over 90 seconds in deep frost zones vitrifies the suit and resets to the nearest beacon.
  - **Core Cracked**: Allowing the central Chamber Bell to take three un-dampened dissonance strikes during stabilization sequences cracks the bell and restarts the encounter.

---

## 4. Interactivity Modes (10 Points)
1. **First-Person Precision Tuning Mode**: Used when aiming light prisms, reading stress fractures through the spectroscopic visor, and striking glass chimes with fine pitch control.
2. **Third-Person Kinetic Traversal Mode**: Automatically transitions during movement—skating across spun-glass ribbons, gliding on thermal updrafts, and leaping between swinging pendulum platforms.
3. **Tactile Blueprint / Acoustic Map Mode**: A diegetic 3D glass-model projection held in the player's palm showing real-time wave propagation across the cavern for macro-level puzzle planning.
4. **Asynchronous Co-Op "Echo Chimes" Mode**: Players can leave tuned *Resonance Vessels* that appear as ghostly harmonic relays in other players' worlds to help extend light beams toward secret chambers.

---

## 5. Challenges (10 Points)
1. **Spatial-Optic Routing Puzzles**: Bending light beams around curved crystal walls, through moving pendulum lenses, and across multi-level chambers without crossing destructive shadow-prism beams.
2. **Real-Time Stress Management**: Crossing self-made glass bridges while rhythmic *Tectonic Pulses* ripple through the cavern, requiring the player to strike counter-frequencies in rhythm to cancel out destructive waves.
3. **Environmental Material Constraints**: Adapting to three distinct glass types—**Soda-Lime Glass** (easy to melt, shatters easily), **Borosilicate Quartz** (resists heat/vibration, reflects light cleanly), and **Obsidian Basalt** (absorbs light, requires bass-frequency sound to move).
4. **Harmonic Leviathan Encounters**: Climbing the exterior scaffolding of massive, out-of-tune clockwork glass automatons to re-tune their internal tuning forks before their footsteps shatter the cavern floor.

---

## 6. Structure (10 Points)
- **Objectives**:
  - *Primary*: Re-ignite the 5 Deep Strata Kilns to restore the Core Furnace.
  - *Secondary*: Thaw frozen *Memory Ampoules* (glass sculptures replaying acoustic holograms of the vanished civilization).
  - *Mastery*: Complete chambers using the minimum number of spun-glass filaments (*Pure Artisan* rating).
- **Flow**: Follows a rhythmic **Tension $\rightarrow$ Resonance $\rightarrow$ Catharsis** cadence—starting in eerie darkness and fragile silence, building into multi-beam acoustic routing, and culminating in a musical crescendo where the cavern illuminates in vibrant stained-glass colors.
- **Duration**: 10–15 minutes per puzzle chamber; 1.5–2 hours per Stratum (6–8 chambers + 1 Leviathan Kiln); 8–10 hours for the main campaign (12–14 hours for 100% completion).
- **Availability**: Single-player PC & Console release (Unity 3D) with optional asynchronous online *Echo Chimes* and full visual-waveform accessibility for deaf/hard-of-hearing players.
- **Relationship**: Chambers within a Stratum are physically and optically linked—igniting Chamber A sends a permanent beam of light through a ceiling aperture into the central Hub Atrium, unlocking counterweights for Chambers B and C.
- **Progression**: Tool-and-knowledge driven rather than stat grinding:
  1. *Stratum 1*: Pitch-Chisel (Basic Resonance & Echolocation)
  2. *Stratum 2*: Molten Spool (Glass-Bridge Extrusion)
  3. *Stratum 3*: Chromatic Splitter (3-Color Thermal Refraction)
  4. *Stratum 4*: Phase-Dampener (Freezing Vibrations in Time)
  5. *Stratum 5*: Full Harmonic Synthesis at the Core Furnace
